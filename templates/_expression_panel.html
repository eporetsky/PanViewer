    How were RNA-seq and DEG data processed?

    Reads were quality-filtered and adapter-trimmed with fastp, aligned to the reference with HISAT2, and gene counts obtained with featureCounts (exon/CDS, GTF annotation). CPM values were computed with edgeR using TMM normalization; stored CPM is rounded to one decimal place. Differential expression used edgeR quasi-likelihood models (glmQLFit, robust) and glmTreat with |log₂FC| ≥ 1 and FDR ≤ 0.05.
